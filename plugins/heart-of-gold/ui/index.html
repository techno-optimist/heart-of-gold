            THE HITCHHIKER’S FIELD GUIDE
            Don’t panic.
            Start small.
            
      
            Heart of Gold is an open-source experiment from ProjectForty2. One curious human sets a mission. A small crew of roles brings back something useful.
            
      
            1. Choose a mission. Give the crew a question, a plan, or an improbably good idea.
            2. Meet the crew. Every cursor has an assignment and something to bring back. Select one to inspect or edit its work.
            3. Engage the drive. Your chat performs the work with its available tools. Native workers appear only when the host actually creates them; guided passes are roles performed by one assistant.
            4. Bring it home. Follow the ship’s log, question a contribution, and read the crew’s report.
            
      
            Movement is a map of the work.
            Cursor motion is presentation, not a recording of mouse activity or a live token stream. Status comes from the chat host. The plugin does not create agents or call models.
            
      
            One flight deck follows the mission. Its latest report is held temporarily on ProjectForty2 for two hours, until expiry or a restart. The towels are metaphorical.
