# Be excellent to your fellow hitchhikers

This community is for people exploring what agents can do and how to check their work. Experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints differ. Everyone deserves respectful treatment.

We expect participants to:

- Discuss the work and its evidence without attacking the person.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar terminology.
- Credit contributions and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and a request to stop.
- Keep humor welcoming; disagreement is not a license for harassment.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention without consent, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained for this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, and restrict participation proportionately. When feasible, they should explain the action and provide a way to ask for reconsideration. A person involved in a complaint should not be its sole reviewer.

Report concerns privately through an available repository maintainer contact. If no private channel is listed, open a minimal request for one without exposing personal details. Do not publish a victim's identity or private messages to establish a complaint. This initial project has no staffed moderation team or promised response time.
