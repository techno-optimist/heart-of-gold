What happens when you deploy?

  1. Shape the crew. Give each role an assignment and something concrete to bring back.
  2. Deploy the drive. Send that plan into your current conversation.
  3. Follow the work. Inspect reported contributions and unresolved questions. Challenge a branch to improve it.

Plans and reported result summaries sent to the tools pass through ProjectForty2’s stateless renderer. It keeps no plugin flight history and makes no model calls. Read the privacy notice.
